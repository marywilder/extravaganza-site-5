Extravaganza started small: a handful of props, a folding table, and a promise to make every event look like nothing else in town. Nearly two decades later, that promise hasn't changed - just the size of the warehouse. Today we're home to thousands of props spanning every theme you can dream up - the largest collection east of the Mississippi - plus an in-house studio that builds the ones nobody's thought of yet. We still think of every event the same way we did on day one: not as a rental order, but as a room waiting to become something unforgettable.
